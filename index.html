RL Post-Training · Agentic Reasoning · Reward Design

I build the post-training stack end to end — verifiable-reward environments, rollout infrastructure, and RL recipes that survive contact with the real distribution. On Physics-R1 that meant taking Qwen2.5-VL-7B from [[31.2]] to [[44.8]] on a held-out olympiad benchmark, past [[GPT-4o at 41.3]], trained on [[64 × H100]] with [[N]] rollouts/sec. I'm now doing the same for tool-use agents, where the reward is harder to specify and much easier to hack.

Staff Applied Scientist at Adobe Foundry — custom generative foundation models for enterprise customers (SFT → DPO → RL, with data curation as the primary lever). Previously Tech Lead at Amazon Video Search (multimodal retrieval + RL post-training, shipped to production) and GenAI Live Action Studio, and Senior Research SDE at Google Research. PhD from UNC-Chapel Hill with Prof. Ming C. Lin on learning physical parameters from video.
